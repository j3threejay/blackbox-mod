Blackbox colour themes v2 (mock-ups)

Each theme only swaps the 34 palette colours. Screens are approximations at the real 320 x 240, cut to the screen's 15-bit colour. Known issue: stock writes the piano roll's pad numbers in the background colour, so on most themes they vanish; a real theme needs one small code change to give that text its own colour (the mock-ups show stock behaviour).

Displays of old machines

1. Green LCD

Yellow-green backlit LCD with dark ink, like the 80s samplers and drum machines. Lit = inverted.

2. Grey-green LCD

The cooler, greyer backlit LCD of the big-screen MPC-era boxes. Lit = inverted.

3. Blue LCD

Negative blue LCD: deep blue glass, pale blue-white pixels.

4. Orange LCD

Orange backlight, black ink. Loud but very readable.

5. Amber VFD

Gas-plasma / VFD amber: black glass, glowing amber, a hotter orange for selection.

6. Cyan VFD

Vacuum-fluorescent blue-green: black glass, teal glow.

7. Red LED

Seven-segment red: black, red glow, orange for selection.

8. Phosphor

Green-screen terminal: green-black, green glow, lime selection.

Colours

10. JOEBOX

Cyan moved to the logo blue, pink becomes a warm amber. Greys as stock.

11. Club Dark

Darker greys, orange instead of pink, a deeper teal. Easier on the eyes in a dark room.

12. Midnight

Navy-tinted greys, violet for lit, mint for selection.

13. Ember

Charcoal, red for lit, gold for selection.

14. Forest

Green-grey, lime for lit, coral for selection.

15. Ocean

Slate blue, aqua for lit, coral for selection.

16. Rose

Plum greys, pink for lit, sky blue for selection.

17. High Contrast

For daylight: black, near-black squares, yellow lit, red selection.
